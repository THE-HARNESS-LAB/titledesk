# Evaluating TitleDesk

TitleDesk is licensed software. Two evaluations are available from
[title-desk.com/download](https://title-desk.com/download/):

- **The 7-day Individual trial.** Every feature, one computer, free for 7 days. Your
  card is verified with a $1 charge that is refunded immediately; cancel any time from
  [title-desk.com/manage](https://title-desk.com/manage/). The activation code appears
  on the next page and in your email.
- **The 14-day Enterprise demo.** Free, no card. Spencer calls you to set it up, walks
  through the workflow and troubleshoots; the demo runs 14 days from the first
  activation on up to 5 computers.

Deploying across a land department? Email
**[sales@theharnesslab.com](mailto:sales@theharnesslab.com)** and we will set it up
rather than issuing individual trials.

## How a trial licence behaves

- **Time-limited.** It stops working when the term ends and asks for a new
  activation code.
- **Tied to your computer.** The activation binds to the machine you activate
  on. Tell us if you need to move it.
- **The same build.** A trial runs the same signed release as a paid seat; its
  time limit is the only licensing difference.
- **Works offline.** Once activated, TitleDesk keeps working without a network
  connection for an extended period, so a trial in a courthouse basement does
  not fail.

## Activating

1. Install TitleDesk — see the guide for
   [Windows](install-windows.md), [macOS](install-macos.md) or
   [Linux](install-linux.md).
2. Open the activation link from your email **on the computer where TitleDesk is
   installed**, or paste the code into the activation screen.
3. The activation link is single-use and remains redeemable until the deadline
   stated in your invitation. If it has expired, email
   [sales@theharnesslab.com](mailto:sales@theharnesslab.com) for a new link.

Activation email comes from **`licenses@theharnesslab.com`**. If it has not
arrived after ten minutes, check spam, then email us.

## What we can see during a trial

Almost nothing, by design.

The licence server records that a licence was activated and on which device
fingerprint. **It does not receive your documents, your project data, your
ownership calculations or your reports.** Optional AI, Drive and connector
transfers are separate and are described in
[security and privacy](security-and-privacy.md).

## After the trial

If TitleDesk earns its place, plans and checkout are on the
[product site](https://title-desk.com/#buy). Your projects
carry straight over — a paid licence activates on the same installation and the
same data.

If it does not, the licence expires and TitleDesk switches to restricted,
read-only operation. Existing work remains readable and exportable. There is
nothing to cancel.

## Evaluating across a land department

If several landmen are trialling at once, or your company wants to test a
managed configuration — policy, approved research sources, security posture
distributed to each landman — say so in your email and we will set it up rather
than issuing separate individual trials.
