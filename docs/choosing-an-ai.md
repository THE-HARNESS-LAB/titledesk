# Choosing your AI

**You can also choose none.** TitleDesk reads documents, builds the runsheet,
computes ownership, tracks deadlines and produces reports with no AI connected
at all. Connecting one only adds drafting assistance. If your land department
cannot send client documents to a third party, run TitleDesk with the AI off, or
use a local model (option 3).

TitleDesk works with four kinds of AI. You can switch anytime — your project
data never changes when you switch.

1. **Codex subscription** — uses your existing ChatGPT/Codex plan. TitleDesk
   opens the official sign-in and never sees your password or tokens.
2. **Claude subscription** — uses your Claude Pro/Max plan via the official
   Claude sign-in. Same rule: TitleDesk never reads stored credentials.
3. **Ollama (private/offline)** — everything runs on your computer. Quality depends
   on the model you download, and big models need a recent machine. TitleDesk
   warns you before large downloads.
4. **API key** — for Anthropic, OpenAI, Moonshot/Kimi, or a custom endpoint.
   Keys are stored in your computer's keychain only.

Whichever you pick, the AI gets the same restricted toolset: it can read your
approved project files, search approved websites, and propose findings — it
cannot touch anything else on your computer.
